# CUT: image prompts

Use the style-lock line at the start of **every** prompt so the set stays consistent.

**Style lock:** Flat editorial graphic on a cool graphite background (#14171A). Only two colours besides the background: signal red (#E5352B) and bone (#EDE6D8). Thick uniform shapes, hard edges, no gradients, no glow, no shadows, no texture, no text unless the prompt asks for it. One recurring motif: a single diagonal cut that splits a shape into two halves that slip apart.

**Never include:** people or faces, streamers, game characters, game logos or scenes, palm trees, neon pink or purple, TikTok or other platform logos, any real brand.

---

## 1. Polished logo (1:1)
[Style lock] A single rounded-square slab, cut on the diagonal from bottom-left to top-right. The upper-left half is signal red, the lower-right half is bone. The halves slip apart along the cut with a thin dark gap between them. Centred, lots of empty space around it. No text. Must stay readable at 32 px.

## 2. Avatar (1:1, will be cropped to a circle)
[Style lock] The same cut slab, filling about 55% of the frame, centred on the graphite background so the whole mark survives a circle crop. No ring, no border, no text.

## 3. X banner (3:1, 1500×500)
[Style lock] A long horizontal film-editing timeline seen flat from above: three thin lanes of bone dashes on near-black, a single red vertical playhead line near the right third, and one bone block on the top lane that is cut in two at the playhead, the halves slipping apart. Plenty of empty space on the left for text. No text in the image.

## 4. Icon set (six icons, 1:1 each, same weight and size)
[Style lock] Six pictograms on near-black, bone strokes of equal thickness, one small red detail each, identical canvas and margins:
1. **Cut**: a rounded slab cut on the diagonal.
2. **Post**: an arrow leaving a calendar tile.
3. **Radar**: three concentric arcs with a red dot.
4. **Streamers**: two overlapping rounded rectangles like screens, one red edge.
5. **Feed**: three stacked vertical rectangles, the middle one red.
6. **Earn**: a coin split on the diagonal, one half red.

## 5. Stickers (five, transparent or near-black background, die-cut white border not needed)
[Style lock] Each sticker is one bold shape with a short line of text in a heavy condensed sans (Archivo, condensed), bone on near-black or red:
1. A cut slab with the text "CUT".
2. A red playhead line with the text "TAKE YOUR CUT".
3. Two slipping halves of a rounded square with the text "+3.5%".
4. A bone timecode strip reading "00:00:00:00".
5. A red diagonal slash across a bone rectangle with the text "ROLLING".

## 6. Link preview card (1.91:1, 1200×630)
[Style lock] Left half empty near-black for text. Right half: the cut slab, large, cropped slightly by the right edge. A thin red playhead line runs the full height at the split. No text in the image (the page adds it).
